IFDB (fanedit.org)

Metadata for fanedits from the Internet Fanedit Database. For best results, keep fanedits in their own Movies library and enable IFDB as its first metadata and image provider.

Metadata

Appends sections such as "Editing Details" and "Cuts and Additions" after the synopsis.
For example "FanFix", "Extended" or "Shortcut".
Adds an IMDb link. Leave off if other metadata providers are enabled on the library, or they will fill in the original film's details.

Matching

How fanedit.org matches the searched title.
During library scans an unidentified movie is only matched when exactly one result scores at least this much. Lower it to match more, at the risk of wrong matches.
Results scoring below this are hidden in the Identify dialog.

Connection

https://fanedit.org or https://ifdb.fanedit.org.
How the plugin identifies itself to fanedit.org. Leave empty for the default (Jellyfin-IFDB/ followed by the plugin version).
Spaces out requests during library scans to avoid tripping the site's rate limits.
0 turns caching off.
